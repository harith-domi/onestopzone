FAQ

Common Questions

Do you serve companies outside of Kuala Lumpur?

Yes. We serve businesses across Malaysia and support companies with international operations and cross-border trade requirements.

Can we engage just one service, or do we need to take a package?

You can engage any single service independently. Many clients start with one service and expand as they see results. There's no obligation to bundle services.

How quickly can you deploy manpower or start a project?

Timelines vary by service. For manpower, we can often deploy within a few days. For technology or marketing projects, we begin with a scoping session before setting a project timeline with you.

Is there a minimum contract or engagement period?

This depends on the service. Some services are project-based with no minimum commitment, while ongoing services such as staffing or digital marketing are typically structured on a monthly retainer. Contact us to discuss terms for your specific needs.
